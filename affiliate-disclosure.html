Affiliate Disclosure

Last updated: July 25, 2026

PuzzleHub is free to use, and staying free costs money to run. Part of how we cover that is affiliate marketing: some of the links on this site point to products we recommend, and if you buy something after clicking one, we earn a small commission.

As an Amazon Associate, PuzzleHub earns from qualifying purchases.

What this means in practice

When a blog post recommends a pencil, a printer, or a binding kit for your workbook, that link may be an affiliate link. The price you pay is exactly the same whether you use our link or go to the retailer directly — the commission comes out of the retailer's margin, not your wallet.

How we choose what to recommend

We recommend products we'd suggest regardless of whether they paid us to, based on what actually works for printing and solving puzzles at home or in a classroom. No company pays for placement in our Journal articles, and no affiliate relationship changes which puzzle formats we build or how we write about them.

Where you'll see these links

  • Journal articles about printing, binding, or setting up a workspace for puzzle-solving.
  • Occasional product mentions inside Creator Studio guides, such as recommended paper stock for workbook printing.
  • Nowhere inside the puzzles themselves. The arcade and your library are ad-free.

Other affiliate relationships

Beyond the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, we may occasionally work with other retailers under similar arrangements. Any such relationship will be disclosed the same way: plainly, and before you click.

Questions

If you're ever unsure whether a specific link is an affiliate link, ask us at admin@puzzlehub.site and we'll tell you directly.
