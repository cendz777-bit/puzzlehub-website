How to Play Sudoku

  • Objective: Fill the grid so every row, column, and sub-grid contains all numbers from 1 up to the grid size.
  • Rules for 9×9: Each of the nine 3×3 boxes must contain the digits 1 through 9 without repetition.
  • Classroom & Print Use: Clicking the Print button automatically strips away navigation and footers, leaving only a clean worksheet for offline use.
