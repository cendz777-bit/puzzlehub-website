What to include

A quick note on what you're writing about helps us route it faster:

Account or billing

The email your account is registered under, and whether it concerns the free tier or Creator Studio.

Puzzle correction

The puzzle name and, if you have it, a screenshot of the error — a missing word, a broken grid, an unsolvable clue.

Creator Studio / licensing

What you're publishing and where, so we can confirm your commercial rights are set up correctly.

Press or partnerships

A short description of the outlet or project you're writing on behalf of.
