Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 25, 2026

PuzzleHub is a puzzle site with a free arcade and a paid Creator Studio. This page explains what we collect when you use either one, why we collect it, and what choices you have.

1. What we collect

If you never create an account, we collect almost nothing you'd recognize as personal: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, pages visited) and basic analytics that tell us which puzzles get played and where people give up. That's it.

If you create a free account, we add your email address, the name you register with, and a record of which puzzles you've completed, printed, or downloaded — this is how your streak and library work.

If you upgrade to Creator Studio, we also store your billing name, the last four digits of your payment card, and your subscription status. We never see or store your full card number; that's handled entirely by our payment processor.

If you use Creator Studio to build a workbook, we store the puzzle configurations you generate (theme, palette, grid size, page count) so you can come back and edit them.

2. What we don't collect

We don't ask for your address, phone number, or date of birth. We don't track you across other websites, and we don't build advertising profiles. Nothing on PuzzleHub is sold to data brokers.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use a small number of cookies to keep you signed in and to remember settings like your last-used palette in Creator Studio. We also run privacy-conscious analytics to see aggregate traffic patterns — which pages are read, which puzzles are launched, how long a session lasts. This data is reported in aggregate; we don't use it to identify individual visitors.

4. How your information is used

  • To run your account: saving progress, streaks, and your print/download library.
  • To process Creator Studio subscriptions and generate your workbook exports.
  • To send you account-related email — password resets, receipts, and changes to these policies. We do not send marketing email unless you opt in separately.
  • To keep the site working: diagnosing errors, preventing abuse, and understanding which puzzle formats are worth building next.

5. Who we share it with

We share billing information with our payment processor to complete transactions. We share aggregate, non-identifying analytics with our hosting and analytics providers so the site stays fast. We do not share your email, your library, or your puzzle history with advertisers or third-party marketers.

If required by law — a valid subpoena or court order — we may be compelled to share account information with authorities. We'll notify you unless we're legally barred from doing so.

6. Children's privacy

Some PuzzleHub content, including the tracing and vocabulary workbooks in Creator Studio, is designed for children to use on paper, under the supervision of a parent, teacher, or the adult who purchased the workbook. Accounts on PuzzleHub itself are intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children, and we ask that a parent or guardian create and manage any account used to generate materials for a child.

7. Data retention

We keep account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we remove your personal information within 30 days, though anonymized usage statistics (which can no longer be tied back to you) may be retained indefinitely.

8. Your choices

  • You can review or update your account details at any time from your dashboard.
  • You can request a copy of the data we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by writing to the address below.
  • You can cancel a Creator Studio subscription at any time; you'll keep access through the end of the billing period you've already paid for.

9. Security

Passwords are stored using industry-standard hashing, and billing details never touch our own servers directly — they go straight to our PCI-compliant payment processor. No system is perfectly secure, but we treat account data with the same care we'd want applied to our own.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make a meaningful change to how we handle your data, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, for account holders, send a short notice by email before the change takes effect.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy, or requests related to your data, can be sent to admin@puzzlehub.site.
